USDC on Arc mainnet closed above $1B for the first time on 27 October: 1,009.8M at the last block of the day (UTC). On 30 September it was 458.9M. It has more than doubled in four weeks.

Supply

Over the week, supply went from 820.5M to 1,073.2M: +252.7M. No day since launch on 15–16 September had closed above $1B; the highest close before was 979.8M, on 26 October.

A CCTP or Gateway mint is not issuance: the same dollars changed chains. The two cross-chain routes pulled in opposite directions and netted +66.0M. Organic issuance — USDC created or destroyed on Arc itself, once both routes are taken out — added +186.7M, 74% of the week's growth. How each route is attributed: Bridged liquidity vs. issuance.

Raw counts every Transfer event. Real keeps the largest transfer per transaction, per token, so routing hops and contract internals are not counted twice. Adjusted is real volume minus transfers where both ends are infrastructure. Same chain, same week: raw volume rose by 40% while the number of transfers fell by 20%, and adjusted volume fell.

The infrastructure filter was not capped this week — 271 addresses qualified and all 271 were flagged, against a cap of 20,000 — so the fall in adjusted volume follows from the published rule, not from a limit. Definitions and thresholds: methodology.

Reproduce

Flows and volumes, in daily UTC buckets (free key on /docs):

GET /v1/stablecoins/history?token=USDC&range=30d

Each bucket carries volume, rvolume, avolume, mint and burn; the CCTP and Gateway parts are cmint/cburn and bmint/bburn. Organic issuance is mint − cmint − bmint against burn − cburn − bburn. Supply needs no key: call totalSupply() on the USDC contract at the blocks above, on any Arc mainnet RPC.

Limits

Volumes and flows come from Stabledesk's index; supply is checked against the chain, flows and volumes are not re-read independently for this report. CCTP by counterparty chain is not shown: /v1/cctp serves rolling 24h and 7d windows only, so a fixed week could not be reproduced later. EURC, GBPA and USYC are not covered. Figures were read on 30 October 2026 at about 17:20 UTC; if the index is corrected later, the correction is dated on the methodology page and this report is not edited silently. Not investment advice.
