Reproduce

Flows and volumes, in daily UTC buckets (free key on /docs):

GET /v1/stablecoins/history?token=USDC&range=all

Each bucket carries volume, rvolume, avolume, cnt, mint and burn; the CCTP and Gateway parts are cmint/cburn and bmint/bburn. Organic issuance is mint − cmint − bmint against burn − cburn − bburn; raw volume without mints and burns is volume − mint − burn. The same series is served without a key by the stablecoin_history tool of the MCP connector at https://stabledesk.xyz/mcp, with token USDC and range all.

Supply needs no key: call totalSupply() on the USDC contract at the blocks above, on an Arc mainnet RPC that keeps historical state. End-of-day supply on any other day since launch follows from one of those reads and the same series: subtract each later day's mint − burn.

Limits

Volumes and flows come from Stabledesk's index. Supply is read from the chain and the index's daily net issuance matches it; transfers are not re-read independently for this report, and who minted and burned is not attributed. “Since launch” means since the index's record began, on 15 September 2026: eight weeks of history. A record over “seven complete UTC days” compares windows that start at midnight UTC; a seven-day window starting at another hour can hold more. The flag set moves from minute to minute because the rule converts sent history into days at a block time sampled over the last 15 blocks, from whole-second timestamps: 428.6, 500 or 571.4 ms, against 507.4 ms on average over the week. Adjusted volume carries that noise. CCTP by counterparty chain is not shown: /v1/cctp serves rolling 24h and 7d windows only, so a fixed week could not be reproduced later. EURC, GBPA and USYC are not covered. Each figure is rounded to 0.1M on its own, so a sum or a difference of rounded figures can be off by 0.1M. Figures were read on 13 November 2026, from the index as it stood at 13:07 UTC. If the index is corrected later, the correction is dated on the methodology page; this report stays as published.
