Raw USDC volume on Arc mainnet reached 5,428.3M from 30 October to 5 November, the most in any seven complete UTC days since launch in mid-September, while real volume fell 9.7%. Mints and burns, which raw volume counts and real volume leaves out, account for 94% of raw volume's rise.

Raw counts every Transfer event the token contract emits, mints and burns included. Real keeps the largest transfer per transaction, per token, so routing hops and contract internals are not counted twice, and leaves mints and burns out. Adjusted is real volume minus transfers where both ends are infrastructure. Mints and burns account for 94% of raw volume's rise; without them, raw volume rose 5.0%, and real volume fell.

An address that sent more than 1,000 transfers or $10M in 30 days, or more than that monthly rate over a longer sent history, is infrastructure. The flag set is recomputed every minute, and each minute's adjusted volume uses the set of that minute. Its cap played no part: on 6 November the set held between 229 and 256 addresses from one minute to the next, every qualifying address flagged, against a cap of 20,000.

One caveat on the comparison. Sent history starts on 27 September, so until 27 October an address active before then was judged on less than a month of sending, which flags fewer addresses and puts adjusted volume above where it settles. That covers the previous week's first four days: judged on a full month, they would have counted somewhat less, and the fall would be that much smaller. The fall does not come from them, though: they averaged 31.6M of adjusted volume a day, below this week's 38.1M, while 28 and 29 October averaged 91.5M. Definitions and thresholds: methodology.

A CCTP or Gateway mint is not issuance: the same dollars changed chains. The two cross-chain routes pulled in opposite directions, as the week before (CCTP +418.1M, Gateway −352.1M), and netted +80.1M.

Organic issuance — USDC created or destroyed on Arc itself, once both routes are taken out — netted +33.9M: 30% of the week's growth, against 74% (+186.7M) the week before. Gross, 1,348.0M was created and 1,314.1M destroyed, the most on both sides in any seven complete UTC days since launch and 70% of the week's mints and burns. Reproduce

Flows and volumes, in daily UTC buckets (free key on /docs):

GET /v1/stablecoins/history?token=USDC&range=all

Each bucket carries volume, rvolume, avolume, cnt, mint and burn; the CCTP and Gateway parts are cmint/cburn and bmint/bburn. Organic issuance is mint − cmint − bmint against burn − cburn − bburn; raw volume without mints and burns is volume − mint − burn. The same series is served without a key by the stablecoin_history tool of the MCP connector at https://stabledesk.xyz/mcp, with token USDC and range all.

Supply needs no key: call totalSupply() on the USDC contract at the blocks above, on an Arc mainnet RPC that keeps historical state. End-of-day supply on any other day since launch follows from one of those reads and the same series: subtract each later day's mint − burn.

Limits

Volumes and flows come from Stabledesk's index. Supply is read from the chain and the index's daily net issuance matches it; transfers are not re-read independently for this report, and who minted and burned is not attributed. “Since launch” means since the index's record began, on 15 September 2026: seven weeks of history. A record over “seven complete UTC days” compares windows that start at midnight UTC; a seven-day window starting at another hour can hold more. The flag set moves from minute to minute because the rule converts sent history into days at a block time sampled over the last 15 blocks, from whole-second timestamps: 429, 500 or 571 ms, against about 507 ms on average. Adjusted volume carries that noise. CCTP by counterparty chain is not shown: /v1/cctp serves rolling 24h and 7d windows only, so a fixed week could not be reproduced later. EURC, GBPA and USYC are not covered. Each figure is rounded to 0.1M on its own, so a sum or a difference of rounded figures can be off by 0.1M. Figures were read on 6 November 2026 between about 08:50 and 10:10 UTC. If the index is corrected later, the correction is dated on the methodology page; this report stays as published.
