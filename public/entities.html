This page is in beta

Classifications are derived automatically and are not verified. A type tells you what an address behaves like on-chain — it is not a claim about who operates it. Expect gaps: an address doing nothing recognisable is simply shown as contract, and testnet data is not representative of mainnet.

The method and thresholds may change while this is being tested. Don't build anything on these results yet — the stable API is the one to rely on.
