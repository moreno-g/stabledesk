This registry grows from evidence

A protocol is listed here only when its contracts are verified on-chain or confirmed by the team that operates it — nothing is listed on a guess. Entries marked verified were confirmed by the operator; observed means we found the contract and classified it ourselves, which is not a claim about who runs it.

Contracts holding real balances that we cannot attribute appear under Unnamed contracts below, with their value counted in the chain total but never folded into a protocol. If you operate one, or can identify one, that's the fastest way onto this page — see PROTOCOLS.md or @getStabledesk. Testnet figures are faucet money and are not representative of mainnet.

Unnamed contracts

Contracts holding stablecoin balances that no registry entry claims. Their value is counted in the chain total above — hiding them would understate the chain, and assigning them to a plausible protocol would invent data. This is the work queue, not a rounding error: today it holds most of the value on Arc. Recognise one? Open a PR — every entry has to be checkable against the chain.
